Plan your month, mark full-day absences and holidays, and record individual missed lectures. Attendance updates automatically.

No lecture schedule is prefilled. Enter your own lecture counts for each weekday, then choose any working Saturdays for this month.

Choose how many Saturdays are working and the number of lectures per working Saturday, then tap Apply working Saturdays. You can adjust individual Saturday dates in the calendar. The starting values are 0.

1. Edit weekday lecture counts

Enter the number of lectures you actually have on each weekday. These values apply to every matching weekday in the selected month. All values start at 0; nothing is preset.

3. Mark absences by weekday (X1, X2, X3…)

Choose a weekday and how many times the student missed that weekday this month. Example: Monday ×2 marks two Mondays absent. The tool automatically picks dates from that weekday in the selected month; you can change the exact dates in the calendar afterwards.

4. Monthly calendar

Tap a date to edit it. Full-day absence and official holiday cannot both apply to the same date.

5. Edit selected date

Select a date in the calendar

Use Schedule adjustment when the timetable changes for this specific date: enter a positive number to add lectures (e.g. +2) or a negative number to remove them (e.g. −1). The adjusted total is used in attendance calculations. For a full-day absence, all lectures scheduled that day are deducted from attended lectures. Individual missed lectures are for specific classes missed while you attended the rest of the day.

Calculation: Attendance % = attended lectures ÷ conducted lectures × 100. Official holidays reduce conducted lectures. Full-day absences and individual missed lectures reduce attended lectures. To avoid double-counting, individual missed lectures on a full-day absence or official holiday are ignored.
